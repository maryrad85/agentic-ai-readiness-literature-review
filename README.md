# Organizational Readiness for Agentic AI Adoption in Digital Transformation

A structured narrative (integrative) literature review on what organizations need in place to adopt **agentic AI**: software agents that pursue goals, plan multi-step actions, and act with limited human supervision.

*University of Ottawa — DTI 7101, Research Workshop in Digital Transformation and Innovation (Assignment 3, October 2026).*

---

## Research Questions

**Primary:** What organizational readiness factors most influence whether firms can successfully adopt agentic AI as part of digital transformation?

- **(a)** How do culture, structure, and skills shape the implementation and outcomes of autonomous AI agents?
- **(b)** What governance and change-management practices support or hinder the safe delegation of decisions to agents?

**Scope:** organization-level, peer-reviewed studies published mainly between 2018 and 2026, plus a small number of earlier foundational works.

## Themes

1. **Readiness is multidimensional, and technology is the smallest part.** Technological and data readiness, organizational capability and skills, and cultural readiness and change mindset.
2. **What makes agentic AI different is delegation.** Autonomy and control mechanisms, and agent capabilities and performance limits.
3. **Governance and change management turn readiness into adoption.** Transformation governance and strategy, and change-management and adoption practices.

## Key Finding

Successful agentic-AI adoption appears to depend on **two layers of readiness**:

| Layer | Factors | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|
| **Base layer** (AI in general) | Strategic alignment and top-management support, data quality, AI capability (technical + business skills, cross-functional coordination), and a culture committed to change | Strong empirical support |
| **Agentic layer: *delegation readiness*** | Well-documented processes with little process debt, explicit autonomy levels per task, oversight and override mechanisms, and clear accountability for agent actions | Largely conceptual so far |

The base layer is necessary but not sufficient. Delegation readiness is the factor most specific to agentic AI, and it is where future empirical work is most needed.
